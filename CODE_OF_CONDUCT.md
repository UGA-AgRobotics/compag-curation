# Code Of Conduct

## Our Standard

Project participants are expected to communicate professionally, review ideas
on their technical merits, respect differing experience and perspectives, and
help maintain an environment free from harassment and discrimination.

Acceptable behavior includes:

- giving specific, actionable, and respectful feedback;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting errors;
- protecting research participants, collaborators, and private project data;
- respecting licensing, authorship, and attribution boundaries;
- focusing disagreements on code, evidence, and documented requirements.

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks;
- publishing another person's private information;
- sharing credentials, private research assets, or restricted evidence;
- deliberate misrepresentation of scientific results or software capability;
- sustained disruption of project discussion or review.

## Scope

This standard applies in project repositories, issue discussions, reviews,
project communication channels, and public events where a participant
represents the project.

## Reporting And Enforcement

Use a repository-host private contact route when one is available. Otherwise,
open a sanitized public issue requesting a private route and include no
sensitive details. Once that route is established, provide enough context for
a fair review while minimizing unnecessary personal information.

Maintainers may remove content, restrict participation, or take other
proportionate action. They will avoid conflicts of interest and handle reports
as confidentially as practical. Retaliation against a person who reports a
concern in good faith is not acceptable.
